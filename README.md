# E-Commerce Delivery & Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
Comprehensive analysis of Olist E-Commerce data focusing on delivery performance, product sales, seller revenue, and business trends using Python and data visualization.

![Dashboard Preview](assets/dashboard_preview.png)

## Key Insights
- **Delivery Performance**: Average delivery time of X days with Y% on-time delivery
- **Top Products**: Identified 10 best-selling products accounting for Z% of total sales
- **Seller Revenue**: Top sellers generate R$X million in revenue
- **Seasonal Trends**: Revenue peaks during holiday seasons

## 📈 Analysis Sections

### 1. Delivery Time Analysis
- Distribution of delivery times across all orders
- Identification of delivery performance outliers
- Customer experience impact assessment

### 2. Top Selling Products
- Identification of most popular products by quantity sold
- Price analysis of top-performing items
- Market basket analysis opportunities

### 3. Seller Performance
- Revenue ranking of top-performing sellers
- Freight cost vs product price analysis
- Seller concentration and market share

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trends
- Seasonal pattern identification
- Growth rate calculations
- Marketing strategy recommendations

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Python 3.8+**
- **Pandas** - Data manipulation and analysis
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** - Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** - Interactive analysis environment
